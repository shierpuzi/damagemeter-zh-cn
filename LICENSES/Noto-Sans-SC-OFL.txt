Note on the merged faces in ../fonts/
------------------------------------

Those files are **derived from the mod author's own font assets** (the Minecraft
Seven / Minecraft Five Bold faces shipped inside Damage Meter) with the Noto Sans
SC CJK outlines added. They are provided to make it trivial for the author to
test Chinese rendering, and they are redistribution-neutral for everyone else
only in the sense that both inputs were already redistributable:

  * the CJK outlines are OFL 1.1 (above);
  * the Minecraft-style outlines are the author's own assets, and this repository
    makes no claim over them.

If the author would rather not take pre-built binaries, delete `../fonts/` and
regenerate them from their own assets with `../tools/merge_cjk_font.py`; the
script needs the original CFF assets plus a local copy of Noto Sans SC.
